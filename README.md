Key Insights
The United Kingdom accounts for the largest share of sales.
Sales vary across different months, showing noticeable seasonal patterns.
A small number of products contribute significantly to total sales and quantity.
The scatter plot shows the relationship between quantity and transaction amount.
Cancellation transactions represent a smaller portion of overall transactions.
Interactive Power BI filters allow users to explore the data by year and country.
